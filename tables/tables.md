# Tables — Six Sigma DMAIC Report

### Table 1: Project Scope — Included and Excluded Items
| Included in Scope | Excluded from Scope |
|---|---|
| Checkout process | Inventory management |
| Queue management | Store layout |
| Billing activities | Online orders |
| Packing process | Staffing budget and recruitment |
| Customer waiting time | Product pricing |

### Table 2: Measure Phase — Key Performance Indicators
| KPI | Result | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Average Waiting Time | 10.29 min | Customers wait more than 10 minutes on average before service begins. |
| Average Billing Time | 3.42 min | The billing transaction itself takes over 3 minutes on average. |
| Average Folding & Packing Time | 1.58 min | Folding and packing add additional service time at the checkout counter. |
| Maximum Waiting Time | 14.78 min | During peak periods, customers may wait nearly 15 minutes. |
| Minimum Waiting Time | 7.00 min | Even the shortest observed waiting time is relatively high. |
| Standard Deviation | 2.05 min | Waiting times vary by approximately 2 minutes, indicating inconsistent service performance. |

### Table 3: 5 Whys Analysis
| Why Level | Analysis |
|---|---|
| Problem | Customers experience long waiting times during peak shopping hours. |
| Why 1? | Because each customer spends a long time at the checkout counter. |
| Why 2? | Because the cashier performs multiple tasks, including scanning, removing security tags, folding clothes, packing purchases, processing payment, and printing receipts. |
| Why 3? | Because there is no dedicated packing staff or support during busy periods. |
| Why 4? | Because the checkout process has not been optimized for peak-hour demand, and only two billing counters are available. |
| Why 5? | Because staffing and checkout operations are planned similarly for normal and peak business periods, creating a bottleneck during high customer demand. |

### Table 5: Key Performance Indicators — Current vs. Expected

### Table 6: Control Plan
| KPI | Target | Monitoring Frequency | Responsible Person | Corrective Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Average Waiting Time | < 7 min | Weekly | Store Manager | Review staffing during peak hours |
| Billing Time | < 3 min | Weekly | Checkout Supervisor | Observe cashier performance and provide coaching |
| Queue Length | < 5 customers | Daily (Peak Hours) | Shift Supervisor | Open an additional billing counter if required |
| Customer Satisfaction | > 90% | Monthly | Store Manager | Review customer feedback and implement improvements |
